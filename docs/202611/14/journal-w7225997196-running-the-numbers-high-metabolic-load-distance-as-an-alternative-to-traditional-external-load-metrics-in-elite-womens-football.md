## 摘要
摘要 本研究考察了女子足球中高代谢负荷距离（HMLD）与session RPE（sRPE）及运动员身心状态之间的关系，并将其与传统负荷监测指标进行比较。采用观察性设计，收集一支精英女子足球队在2024/25赛季澳大利亚A‐League W训练和比赛期间的数据。24名运动员佩戴全球定位设备以采集外部负荷指标，包括总距离（TD）、高速跑距离（HSRD）和HMLD。通过在线问卷收集sRPE和每日身心状态。使用广义相加模型检验外部负荷与随后数日身心状态及RPE之间的非线性关系，以及晨间身心状态与当日外部负荷之间的关系。较高的负荷与sRPE升高及次日身心状态较差相关，且效应在训练或比赛后2至3天内逐渐减弱。在所有结局中，HMLD比TD和HSRD表现出更强且更一致的关系，可解释身心状态和sRPE更大比例的方差。当日身心状态与距离差异相关，动机较高和疲劳较低对应较高的HMLD。在该队列中，HMLD与即时主观用力感和短期运动员报告的身心状态之间的关联相对强于传统基于距离的指标。这些发现支持将HMLD作为一种实用且敏感的指标，用于指导精英女子足球的训练决策。

## Abstract
ABSTRACT This study examined the relationship between High Metabolic Load Distance (HMLD) and both session RPE (sRPE) and athlete wellness in women's football and compared it with traditional load monitoring metrics. An observational design was used to collect data from one elite women's football team during training and games in the 2024/25 A‐League W season. Twenty‐four athletes wore global positioning devices to capture external load metrics, including total distance (TD), high‐speed running distance (HSRD) and HMLD. Session RPE and daily wellness were collected through online questionnaires. Generalised additive models were used to examine non‐linear relationships between external load, subsequent days' wellness and RPE, and relationships between morning wellness and same‐day external load. Higher load was associated with increased sRPE, and poorer next‐day wellness, with effects diminishing within two to 3 days post‐session. Across all outcomes, HMLD demonstrated stronger and more consistent relationships than TD and HSRD, explaining a greater proportion of variance in wellness and sRPE. Same‐day wellness was associated with differences in distances, with higher motivation and lower fatigue corresponding to higher HMLD. HMLD demonstrated comparatively stronger associations with immediate perceived exertion and short‐term athlete reported wellness than traditional distance‐based metrics in this cohort. These findings support the application of HMLD as a practical and sensitive measure to inform training decision‐making in elite women's football.
