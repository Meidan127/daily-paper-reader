## 摘要
目的：探讨英国陆军基础训练期间淘汰和损伤的预测因素，并检验统计学习模型预测这些结局的性能。方法：在训练第1周测量968名男性和450名女性新兵的人口学特征、生活习惯、情绪、体能表现、身体成分、微量营养素状态和骨代谢。采用逻辑回归评估预测因素与训练结局（淘汰；下肢过度使用性肌肉骨骼损伤）之间的关联。采用逻辑回归、弹性网、随机森林和梯度提升预测训练结局。结果：66%的男性和76%的女性通过训练，其中分别有20%和34%发生损伤。对于男性，2.4 km跑时间较慢（比值比[OR]=1.004，p=0.011）和抑郁症状较多（OR=1.173，p=0.001）与较高的淘汰风险相关，而面积骨密度较低（OR=0.008，p=0.002）、2.4 km跑时间较慢（OR=1.006，p=0.011）、既往应力性骨折（OR=3.267，p=0.002）、甲状旁腺激素较低（OR=0.783，p=0.009）和I型胶原交联C-末端肽（βCTX）较低（OR=0.255，p=0.041）与较高的损伤风险相关。对于女性，2.4 km跑时间较慢（OR=1.009，p=0.009）和总25-羟基维生素D较高（OR=1.013，p=0.001）与较高的淘汰风险相关，而2.4 km跑时间较慢（OR=1.007，p=0.009）、酒精摄入量较高（OR=1.006，p=0.017）和βCTX较低（OR=0.057，p=0.026）与较高的损伤风险相关。男性和女性训练结局的预测性能均较差（受试者工作特征曲线下面积≤0.68）。所有模型均具有低灵敏度和高特异度，但应用基于Youden指数确定的结局特异性阈值可提高灵敏度。结论：改善军事训练结局的干预措施应在开始训练前着眼于有氧适能。预测模型可能有助于识别部分“高危”新兵。

## Abstract
Purpose: Investigate predictors of failure and injury during British Army basic training and test the performance of statistical learning models to predict these outcomes. Methods: 968 male and 450 female recruits had demographics, lifestyle habits, mood, physical performance, body composition, micronutrient status, and bone metabolism measured at week 1 of training. Associations between predictors and training outcome (failure; lower limb overuse musculoskeletal injury) were assessed by logistic regression. Training outcome was predicted by logistic regression, elastic net, random forests, and gradient boosting. Results: 66% of men and 76% of women passed with 20% and 34% developing an injury. For men, slower 2.4 km run time (odds ratio [OR]=1.004, p=0.011) and higher symptoms of depression (OR=1.173, p=0.001) were associated with higher risk of failing, and lower areal bone mineral density (OR=0.008, p=0.002), slower 2.4 km run time (OR=1.006, p=0.011), previous stress fracture (OR=3.267, p=0.002), lower parathyroid hormone (OR=0.783, p=0.009), and lower c-telopeptide cross-links of type I collagen (βCTX) (OR=0.255, p=0.041) were associated with higher risk of injury. For women, slower 2.4 km run time (OR=1.009, p=0.009) and higher total 25-hydroxyvitamin-D (OR=1.013, p=0.001) were associated with higher risk of failing, and slower 2.4 km run time (OR=1.007, p=0.009), higher alcohol intake (OR=1.006, p=0.017), and lower βCTX (OR=0.057, p=0.026) were associated with higher risk of injury. Training outcome was poorly predicted in men and women (area under the receiver operating characteristic curve≤0.68). All models had low sensitivity and high specificity, but application of an outcome-specific threshold determined using the Youden Index improved sensitivity. Conclusions: Interventions to improve military training outcome should address aerobic fitness prior to starting training. Prediction models may be useful in identifying some ‘at-risk’ recruits.
